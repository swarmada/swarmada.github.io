v1.2 · sweep primary · ess retired

Sweep is the mark. Echelon is the icon.

The sweep is the identity: 26 craft on an S-shaped path, the full statement of the idea. The echelon is what the sweep becomes when there is not room for it — the same three machine classes, same headings, three units instead of twenty-six. One rule governs the swap.

Where this still binds. Naming the sweep as primary is a brand decision and it holds everywhere the surface allows. It does not change physics: at 16 px, in a 40 px circular avatar and in a 104×52 landscape tile the sweep has no readable structure, so those three placements take the echelon whatever the guide calls primary. The cncf/artwork icon file — square, no wordmark — is the echelon for the same reason.

The crossover
48 px. Below it, echelon only. Above it, either — choose by whether the surface is wide enough for the sweep's diagonal to breathe. When in doubt, echelon: it is never wrong, only ever less expressive.

What retiring the ess costs and buys

Buys — a materially easier clearance
The letterform was the harder search by an order of magnitude. Objects-forming-letters plus airplanes in the class-009 cluster returned 204 live marks. The formation geometry — airplanes plus four-or-more triangles, same filter — returned 8, none of them in software or robotics. Dropping the ess drops the contested half of the clearance and keeps the clean half.
Buys — one less thing to govern
Two marks with a single numeric crossover is a rule anyone can follow. Three tiers needed a paragraph of explanation and invited drift.
Costs — the name connection
The ess was the only mark that tied the identity to the word Swarmada. Without it the marks are a good picture of a fleet with no link to the name, and the wordmark now carries that job alone. That is the normal arrangement for most projects — but it is a real thing given up, and it is worth knowing it was given up deliberately rather than overlooked.
Kept, not deleted
The ess stays in the repository under alternates/. It is drawn, documented and reproducible from its parameters if it is ever wanted for a wordless application.
