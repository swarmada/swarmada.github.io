# Swarmada Trademark & Logo Policy

Status: **interim, pre-clearance.** The Swarmada name and marks are presently
unregistered. A formal clearance search is in progress — see
`Design_Code_Search_Runbook.md`. This policy states how the marks may be used
in the meantime, under common-law trademark rights; it is not a
representation that registration has been filed or granted, and it will be
superseded if the marks are registered, or if they are donated to a neutral
body (for example, on CNCF Sandbox acceptance).

## What this covers

- The word mark **Swarmada** and the project's `swarmada.io` / `swarmada`
  naming.
- The graphic marks in this directory: the **sweep** (primary mark, 26 craft)
  and the **echelon** (icon mark, 3 craft), in all colorways (`-color`,
  `-black`, `-white`, `-mono`) and the retired `-ess` alternate.

It does not cover the Swarmada source code, which is licensed separately
(see this repository's `LICENSE`, where present). A license to use, modify,
and redistribute Swarmada code does not carry a license to use the Swarmada
name or marks beyond the terms below.

## Permitted, without asking

- Referring to the Swarmada project by name in writing, talks, articles,
  comparisons, and documentation.
- Using an unmodified mark file from this directory to link to, or indicate
  compatibility with, the Swarmada project — for example, a "Built with
  Swarmada" badge, an integration listing, or a Fleet Adapter's own
  documentation — provided the usage does not suggest that Swarmada endorses,
  certifies, or is affiliated with the linking project, product, or company
  beyond that factual relationship.
- Journalistic and educational use, including screenshots of the marks as
  they appear on the project's own site or repositories.

## Not permitted without permission

- Modifying a mark: recoloring it outside the four defined colorways,
  changing its proportions, adding strokes, gradients, outlines, drop
  shadows, or otherwise altering the geometry defined in
  `Swarmada_Mark_System_v1_2.html`.
- Using a Swarmada mark, or anything confusingly similar to one, as your own
  product, company, or project logo.
- Using a Swarmada mark in a way that implies certification, endorsement, or
  official partnership that does not exist.
- Registering a domain, company name, or social handle built on "Swarmada"
  or a confusingly similar term.
- Any commercial merchandise, paid product, or paid service bearing a
  Swarmada mark.
- New use of the retired `-ess` alternate mark (kept in the repository for
  historical/reproducibility reasons only — see `README.txt`).

## Which mark, and how small

The crossover rule, colorways, and minimum sizes are defined in
`README.txt` and `Swarmada_Mark_System_v1_2.html` in this directory. Follow
those specifications exactly; a mark used outside its defined form is
treated as a modification under this policy.
